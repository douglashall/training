# Endurance Training Plans

This repository is a collection of my endurance sport training plans. I use it to plan upcoming seasons, document what I am training for, and share plans that others may find useful.

The plans may cover running, cycling, triathlon, and other endurance goals. Each one is intended to make the progression from the first week of training to race day clear and easy to follow.

## What a plan includes

Where practical, each training plan will describe:

- The target event, distance, or training goal
- The plan duration and expected starting fitness
- Weekly workouts, volume, and recovery days
- Intensity guidance using pace, power, heart rate, or perceived effort
- Important milestones, test sessions, and recovery weeks
- Notes about equipment, fueling, strength work, or mobility

Plans will be grouped by sport and goal as the repository grows. Individual plan files will include any assumptions and terminology needed to use them.

## Using these plans

You are welcome to browse, download, fork, or adapt a plan for your own training. Treat each plan as a starting point rather than a rigid prescription: adjust the volume, intensity, and recovery to suit your experience, schedule, health, and available equipment.

When following a plan:

1. Read the full plan before beginning.
2. Check that its starting workload matches your current fitness.
3. Schedule recovery with the same care as key workouts.
4. Record how sessions feel and adjust when fatigue accumulates.
5. Stop or modify training if pain, illness, or unusual symptoms develop.

## Feedback and contributions

Suggestions, corrections, and ideas are welcome. Feel free to open an issue or submit a pull request if you would like to discuss or improve a plan.

## Disclaimer

These plans reflect personal experience and are shared for informational purposes only. They are not individualized medical or coaching advice. Endurance training carries risk, so consult a qualified healthcare professional or coach when appropriate, particularly before beginning a new program or making a significant increase in training load.
